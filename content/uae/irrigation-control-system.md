## UAE Farming Problem

In UAE agriculture, the main cost is not equipment — it is **water waste**.

- Over-irrigation is common in manual systems
- Manual irrigation lacks precision across crop zones
- Water distribution is inconsistent between greenhouse rows and field blocks

Every liter saved directly improves farm profitability.

## PKYDrip Approach

We design irrigation systems based on **water efficiency logic**, not simple valve control.

The goal is to deliver the right amount of water to each crop zone at the right time — reducing waste while maintaining yield.

## System Capabilities

- Zone-based irrigation control for greenhouses and open fields
- Pump automation for efficient water delivery
- Scheduled irrigation cycles based on crop needs and growth stage
- Water usage optimization per zone — avoid over-watering and runoff

## Ideal Applications

- Greenhouse irrigation
- Vegetable farms
- Date palm farms
- Hydroponic systems

## Value in UAE Context
