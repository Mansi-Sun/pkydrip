## UAE Environmental Challenge

Greenhouses in UAE face extreme heat stress:

- High daytime temperature spikes during summer
- Rapid humidity fluctuations that stress crops
- Cooling inefficiency when ventilation is manual or poorly timed

Unstable climate conditions reduce yield even when irrigation and fertigation are correct.

## PKYDrip Approach

We design climate systems that stabilize greenhouse environment conditions — protecting crops from heat stress and creating predictable growth conditions.

## System Functions

- Fan ventilation control
- Cooling pad activation based on temperature thresholds
- Roof ventilation automation
- Temperature and humidity regulation
- Optional CO₂ optimization support for enhanced growth

## Application Scenarios

- Greenhouse vegetable production
- Vertical farming systems
- Hydroponic cultivation environments
- High-value crop greenhouses (strawberries, herbs, specialty vegetables)

## Value in UAE

- Protect crops from heat stress during peak summer months
- Stabilize growth conditions across seasons
- Improve yield consistency — fewer climate-related crop losses
- Integrate with **[irrigation](/uae/irrigation-control-system/)** and **[fertigation](/uae/fertigation-system/)** for complete farm automation
