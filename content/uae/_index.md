## UAE Agriculture Challenge

- Water is expensive and limited
- High temperature increases evaporation
- Greenhouse farming is dominant
- Fertilizer efficiency is critical for profitability

The core question for UAE farms is not how to build a control system — it is **how to produce more yield using less water**.

## PKYDrip Solution Approach

We design agricultural automation systems focused on:

- **Water efficiency** — reduce unnecessary irrigation and match delivery to crop demand
- **Fertigation precision** — stable nutrient delivery per crop stage
- **Greenhouse climate stability** — protect crops from heat stress and humidity swings
- **Remote farm management** — monitor and adjust systems without constant field presence

## Core Application Areas

- Greenhouse farms
- Hydroponic systems
- Date palm irrigation
- High-value crop farms (vegetables, strawberries, herbs)

## What improves your farm

- Reduce water usage through zone-based irrigation optimization
- Improve fertilizer efficiency with EC / pH monitoring and controlled dosing
- Stabilize greenhouse climate across hot summer seasons
- Reduce manual labor dependency for daily irrigation and fertigation tasks
