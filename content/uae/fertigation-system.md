## UAE Agriculture Challenge

Fertilizer cost and efficiency directly affect profitability in greenhouse farming.

- Nutrient imbalance reduces yield and crop quality
- Over-fertilization increases operating cost without improving output
- Manual mixing is inconsistent across irrigation cycles

## PKYDrip Solution

We provide controlled fertigation systems designed for **precise nutrient delivery per crop stage**.

Integrated with your irrigation schedule, fertigation becomes a predictable part of daily farm operations — not a manual guessing process.

## System Features

- EC monitoring for nutrient concentration
- pH stability control for optimal nutrient uptake
- Controlled dosing injection system
- Integration with irrigation cycles — nutrients delivered when crops need them
