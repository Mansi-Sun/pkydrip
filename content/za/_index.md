## South African Agriculture Challenge

- Unreliable grid electricity in many rural farming areas
- High cost of diesel pumping for irrigation
- Water scarcity requires efficient irrigation delivery
- Existing manual or outdated irrigation systems need retrofit
- Off-grid farms need independent solar pumping solutions

The core question for South African farms is not greenhouse precision — it is **how to run reliable irrigation when electricity is unstable and water is limited**.

## PKYDrip Solution

We provide solar-driven irrigation retrofit systems designed for:

- **Solar pump integration** — energy independence for off-grid and backup operation
- **Irrigation retrofit** — upgrade existing pump and valve systems without full replacement
- **Pump and valve automation** — reliable control when power returns or solar runs
- **Water-efficient delivery** — reduce waste in water-scarce conditions

## Core Applications

- Off-grid crop farms
- Fruit and vegetable farms with unreliable electricity
- Existing irrigation systems needing automation upgrade
- Solar pump projects replacing diesel pumping
- Small to medium commercial farms across South Africa

## Key Value

- Reduce dependency on unstable grid electricity
- Lower diesel pumping costs with solar integration
- Improve irrigation reliability through automated control
- Retrofit existing infrastructure — practical upgrade path
