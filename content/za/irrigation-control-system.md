## South African Farming Problem

Many farms still run manual or basic timer-based irrigation — unreliable and labor-intensive.

- Manual valve operation wastes time and water
- Old pump controllers lack zone sequencing logic
- Retrofit is more practical than full system replacement
- Automation must work with existing pumps and pipe infrastructure

## PKYDrip Approach

We design **irrigation retrofit control systems** — upgrade pump and valve automation on existing farm infrastructure without starting from scratch.

## System Capabilities

- Pump automation retrofit on existing pump sets
- Multi-zone valve control and sequencing
- Scheduled irrigation cycles
- Manual override for field operators
- Integration with solar pump systems

## Applications

- Existing drip and sprinkler irrigation systems
- Farms upgrading from manual to automatic control
- Solar pump projects needing valve coordination
- Small to medium commercial farms
- Fruit, vegetable, and crop farms across South Africa

## South Africa Value

- Practical upgrade path — retrofit, not full rebuild
- Reduce manual labor for daily valve operation
- Improve water delivery consistency across zones
- Works with **[solar irrigation](/za/solar-irrigation-system/)** for complete off-grid operation
