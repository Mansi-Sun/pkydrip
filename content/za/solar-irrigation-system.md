## South African Farming Problem

Many farms depend on grid electricity or diesel for irrigation pumping — both are costly and unreliable.

- Load shedding disrupts scheduled irrigation
- Diesel pumping increases operating cost significantly
- Off-grid farms need independent solar pumping solutions
- Solar panels alone are not enough — pump control integration is critical

## PKYDrip Approach

We design **solar irrigation systems** that integrate solar pumping with irrigation control — not just panels, but a working irrigation system.

## System Capabilities

- Solar pump controller integration
- Hybrid solar + grid + generator operation support
- Pump start/stop automation linked to irrigation schedule
- Water tank level monitoring (optional)
- Valve control coordination with solar pump output

## Applications

- Off-grid crop farms
- Borehole pumping with solar panels
- Farms replacing diesel pumps with solar
- Backup solar systems for grid-connected farms
- Remote farms with no reliable electricity

## South Africa Value

- Energy independence for irrigation operation
- Lower long-term pumping cost vs diesel-only systems
- Irrigation continues during load shedding with solar + storage
- Pair with **[irrigation control](/za/irrigation-control-system/)** for full pump and valve automation
