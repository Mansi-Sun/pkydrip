# PKYDrip Engineering Lab

This is PKYDrip’s public engineering workspace for **irrigation automation**.

## What you can find here
- **Tools**: pump sizing, solar irrigation sizing, pressure/flow quick charts
- **Architecture**: controller → gateway → valves, sensor integration, RS485/Modbus/MQTT notes
- **Logic**: pump + valve sequencing, safety logic, alarms, flow-meter strategies
- **Experiments**: field tests, range tests, cabinet demos, iteration logs
