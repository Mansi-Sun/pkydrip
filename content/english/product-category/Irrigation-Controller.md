## Smart Irrigation Controllers

Modern irrigation is not about one controller for all farms.  
Different projects require different **control architectures**, **power options**, and **levels of automation**.

PKYDrip provides a **modular irrigation controller system**, allowing you to start with a simple setup and expand step by step as your project grows.

---

## Controller Categories

### 🟢 Main Irrigation Controller

The **Main Controller** acts as the central brain of the irrigation system.

**Typical applications:**
- Multi-zone irrigation management  
- Centralized valve and schedule control  
- Integration with sensors and fertigation systems  
- Greenhouses, open fields, and centralized pump rooms  

**Typical products:**
- **PKY-60W Smart Irrigation Controller – Main Controller**

This controller is designed for projects that require stable, centralized irrigation logic and future expansion.

---

### 🟢 Pump Control Controller

The **Pump Control Controller** focuses on managing pumps and power equipment.

**Typical applications:**
- Pump start/stop control  
- Solar pump and hybrid power systems  
- Pressure and dry-run protection (with sensors)  
- Pump stations and main water supply control  

**Typical products:**
- **PKY-60W Smart Irrigation Controller – Pump Control**

This version is suitable when irrigation control and pump operation need to be tightly integrated.

---

### 🔵 Wireless LoRa Irrigation Control System

The **LoRa wireless irrigation system** is designed for large or distributed farms where cabling is difficult.

**Typical applications:**
- Distributed valve control  
- Battery or solar-powered irrigation zones  
- Large farms, orchards, and remote fields  

**System components:**
- **PKY-IC03 Wireless Valve Controller**  
- **PKY-EG08 LoRa–MQTT Gateway**

This system enables long-range, low-power communication between field devices and the central control system.

---

### 🟠 OEM & Modular Irrigation Controllers

For engineering companies, system integrators, and large projects, PKYDrip provides **OEM and modular control solutions**.

**Typical applications:**
- Custom irrigation control panels  
- Multi-zone valve control systems  
- Private-label and ODM projects  

These solutions are designed to integrate flexibly with different irrigation layouts and power conditions.

---

## How to Choose the Right Irrigation Controller

Choosing the right irrigation controller depends on:
- Farm size and layout  
- Water source and pump configuration  
- Power supply (grid, solar, or hybrid)  
- Level of automation required  

There is no one-size-fits-all controller.  
A modular design helps reduce initial cost and allows future upgrades.
