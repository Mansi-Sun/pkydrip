# Partner With PKYDrip in the United States

PKYDrip is expanding local partnerships in the **United States**.  
We support **irrigation contractors, system integrators, and agricultural service companies** with modular automation products and **ready-to-install control cabinets** for real projects.

**Key idea:** We provide standardized control building blocks and cabinet-level delivery.  
You deliver installation, commissioning, and service locally.

---

## Who This Partner Program Is For

This is a fit if your company:

- Delivers irrigation projects (farm / ranch / orchard / greenhouse / landscape)
- Handles pump/valve wiring or works with electrical partners
- Wants to add **pump automation + multi-zone scheduling** without building systems from scratch
- Can support local service and basic troubleshooting

> This is not a consumer retail program. We prefer project-capable companies.

---

## What We Provide

### 1) Ready-to-Install Control Cabinets
- Pre-wired, tested, and field-ready
- Integrated **pump start/stop** (relay or VFD interface)
- Multi-zone sequential irrigation logic
- Designed for scalable projects (expand by adding modules)

### 2) Modular Control Products (Project-Based)
- Pump + valve integrated control
- Multi-zone scheduling & sequencing
- Long-range wireless options (LoRa where suitable)
- Optional sensing for verification (flow/pressure)

### 3) Engineering Support (Remote)
- System architecture guidance
- Wiring diagrams, cabinet layout, commissioning steps
- Remote support during deployment
- Project configuration based on site requirements

---

## Typical USA Use Cases
- Ranch / farm properties using long main lines and multiple branch zones
- Multi-zone drip or sprinkler systems with sequential operation
- Pump automation to reduce manual switching and protect pressure stability
- Projects requiring repeatable cabinet solutions for multiple sites
