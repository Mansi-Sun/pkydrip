## Get Product Information Before Ordering

Tell us the equipment or model, quantity, destination country and a short description of your application. If you are unsure of the model, send a photo of your existing equipment or describe what you need to control.

We will help identify the model and confirm the configuration needed for a quotation. Request specifications, connection information and available operating guidance for that configuration.

[Browse technical guides below](#technical-guides) · [Select equipment by application](/solution/)

## Already Own PKYDrip Equipment?

### Installation and First Use

Contact us for the available wiring, connection and setup information for your model and supplied configuration. Include the model number and a photo of the equipment label so we can identify the right information.

### Everyday Operation and Maintenance

Ask about operating settings, irrigation schedules, dosing functions or routine maintenance for your equipment. Describe what you want to do and include a photo of the relevant screen when helpful.

### Troubleshooting

Send the model, order reference or serial number if available, the alarm message and a short description of what happened. Photos or a short video can help us understand the issue. Avoid changing electrical wiring before confirming the model-specific instructions with a qualified installer.
