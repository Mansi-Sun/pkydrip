    <h2>Sell and Support PKYDrip Equipment in Your Local Market</h2>
    <p class="lead mb-4">
      PKYDrip works with irrigation contractors, greenhouse companies, agricultural equipment dealers and automation integrators that have the ability to serve their local market.
    </p>
    <p>
      PKYDrip focuses on irrigation controllers, fertigation machines, EC/pH control equipment, wireless valve-control products and greenhouse controls. We provide standard equipment, partner pricing, documentation, training and remote technical support. The local partner undertakes the sales, implementation, installation and service responsibilities defined for its cooperation level.
    </p>
    <p><strong>New cooperation starts non-exclusively with a paid sample, demo unit or customer project. Distributor status and territory protection are never granted automatically.</strong></p>
  </section>

  <section class="custom-section">
    <h3>Why Partners Use the PKYDrip Platform</h3>
    <p>Building an irrigation controller or fertigation product from zero usually requires repeated equipment selection, hardware integration, software development, field testing, redesign and production validation. The process can take years, and every change in pumps, valves, sensors or local operating requirements can create another development cycle.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><p><strong>Reduce development time:</strong> use PKYDrip's field-tested hardware, verified device combinations and mature supply chain to shorten a multi-year development path to months. For suitable standard or modular configurations, a working market-evaluation prototype may be prepared in weeks after the specification is confirmed.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Reduce team and continuity risk:</strong> partners do not need to maintain a complete hardware, embedded-software, cloud and commissioning team from the first day. Documented interfaces and stable modules reduce the damage caused by staff turnover or fragmented supplier responsibility.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Build instead of remain dependent:</strong> PKYDrip uses an open, modular architecture. Partners can begin with a configured PKYDrip system, add local functions and branding, and gradually form their own product capability and intellectual property under the agreed development and ownership terms.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Protect confidential plans:</strong> an NDA can be signed before confidential requirements are exchanged. Cooperation can start with PKYDrip customization and manufacturing, then move toward customer-managed assembly or production when the technical package, quality responsibility, volume and transfer terms are agreed in writing.</p></li>
    </ul>
    <p><strong>The objective is faster market entry with a controlled path toward the partner's own brand and engineering capability — not permanent dependence on a closed black-box product.</strong></p>
  </section>

  <section class="custom-section">
    <h3>Commercial Policy</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><p><strong>Partner pricing:</strong> issued after company and market review. Prices are quoted by model, configuration, quantity and destination.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>MOQ:</strong> applies to distributor and OEM orders and is confirmed for each product family and configuration. A paid evaluation unit may be ordered before a batch order.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Samples and demo units:</strong> complete controllers, fertigation machines and other finished equipment are normally paid evaluation orders. Selected control boards, electronic modules or other intelligent components may be provided as evaluation samples under separately agreed written terms. No general free-sample promise applies.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Lead time:</strong> is confirmed in the quotation after the BOM, software options, branding and quantity are approved.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Payment, freight and taxes:</strong> follow the written quotation or pro forma invoice. Import duties, local taxes and local compliance are the buyer's responsibility unless otherwise agreed.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>OEM work:</strong> branding, enclosure, interface, packaging, private deployment and special integration require technical review, an agreed specification and MOQ.</p></li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="custom-section">
    <h3>Product Customization and White-Label Service</h3>
    <p>PKYDrip standard products are the starting point. After the application, target market, technical requirements, forecast volume and responsibility boundary are confirmed, both parties may sign a development agreement and create a new configuration or model for the customer's market.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><p><strong>Requirement confirmation:</strong> functions, interfaces, enclosure, branding, documents, validation method and commercial targets are written into the approved specification.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Development agreement:</strong> new hardware, software or mechanical work begins only after the development scope, charges, MOQ, ownership and delivery stages are agreed in writing.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Confidentiality:</strong> an NDA can be signed before confidential customer, market or product information is exchanged.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>White label:</strong> approved products may use the partner's brand, interface, packaging and documentation under the agreed specification.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Product continuity:</strong> PKYDrip maintains the agreed core platform and provides controlled product and software upgrades. Compatibility and upgrade scope are defined by product version and agreement.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Market protection:</strong> protection for a white-label model or local market must be proportional to actual sales volume, service coverage, investment and agreed business targets.</p></li>
    </ul>
    <p>Customization does not mean unlimited free development. Standard configurations are preferred; special development requires a defined business case and written contract.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="custom-section">
    <h3>Territory Protection Policy</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><p><strong>Evaluation stage:</strong> cooperation is non-exclusive. Both companies verify product fit, communication and delivery capability through a paid order or real project.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Active partner stage:</strong> repeat orders, local promotion and service capability can qualify the partner for project registration, lead coordination and improved commercial terms.</p></li>
      <li><p><strong>Protected territory stage:</strong> a defined territory may be protected only through a written agreement with product scope, duration, sales targets, implementation and service coverage, investment obligations and review dates.</p></li>
    </ol>
    <p><strong>Territory protection is performance-based, time-limited and reviewed regularly.</strong> It can be changed or withdrawn if agreed targets or service obligations are not maintained.</p>
  </section>

    <ol>

      <li><p><strong>Partner review:</strong> agree repeat-order terms, training, market plan and any project or territory protection in writing.</p></li>
