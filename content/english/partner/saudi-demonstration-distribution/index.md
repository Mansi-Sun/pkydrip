## Program Statement

We are building **Saudi Demonstration & Distribution Partners** — selecting **1–2 local partners per season**.

**Entry point** (farm-ready, scalable):
- **Solar water pumping** (solar pump or solar VFD)
- **Auto tank refill** (level sensor / float switch)
- **Optional hybrid solar + grid** operation (boost on peak season / cloudy days / emergency)
- **Irrigation controller** to run zones **sequentially** (stable pressure, lower labor cost)

> Important: This is **not** a “free product” offer.  
> It is a **technical collaboration**: special partner pricing + technical support **in exchange for case documentation** and long-term cooperation.

---

## Why This Matters in Saudi Farms (Short Summary)

Saudi agriculture operates under arid conditions, where **irrigation efficiency and reliable pumping** are critical.  
A practical “**pump + tank + zones**” foundation reduces risk and allows step-by-step upgrades based on real farm needs.

---

## Who We Are Looking For

1) **Operating Farms (10–80 acres)**  
Upgrading an existing irrigation setup (not hobby use)

2) **Irrigation Contractors**  
Want a proven automation package to sell and deliver

3) **Agricultural Equipment Dealers**  
Interested in building a local demonstration point

---

## What We Offer

- **Special partner pricing** (project-based)
- **Technical support** (remote troubleshooting + documentation templates)
- **Case documentation** templates (photos, basic parameters, system logic)
- Optional: **co-branded exposure** on our website/materials (only with your approval)

---

## What We Expect

- Real operating project (location, crop type, water source)
- Pump and power information (kW/HP, voltage, solar/grid)
- Agreement to provide basic case materials (photos/videos, simple numbers)
- Long-term mindset, professional communication

---

## System Logic (Simple + Scalable)

- **Water production layer**: Pump → Tank (auto refill rules)
- **Pressure layer**: booster / pressure control (optional)
- **Distribution layer**: main line → branches → zones
- **Automation layer**: schedules + sequential logic + safety (timeouts / dry-run protection / alarms)
