# Sequential Irrigation Logic

![Sequential Valve Logic](/images/demo/pkydrip-demo-sequential-logic.svg)

The PKYDRIP controller runs irrigation zones **sequentially**.

Example:

Zone 1 → irrigation  
Zone 2 → irrigation  
Zone 3 → irrigation  

Only one valve runs at a time.

This provides:

- stable pressure
- smaller pump requirement
- lower pipeline cost
- reliable irrigation operation

# Protection Functions

The platform demonstrates system safety logic.

### Over Pressure Protection
The system detects abnormal pipeline pressure and stops irrigation automatically.

### Low Flow Protection
Low flow may indicate water shortage or pipeline blockage.

The controller will stop the system to prevent damage.
