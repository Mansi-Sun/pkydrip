# Partner With PKYDrip in Brisbane (QLD)

PKYDrip is expanding local partnerships in **Brisbane / Queensland**.  
We work with **irrigation companies, agricultural service providers, electrical contractors, and system integrators** to deliver real projects—faster, safer, and more repeatable.

**Key idea:** We provide modular control products and **ready-to-install control cabinets**.  
You provide local installation, commissioning, and service.

---

## Who This Partner Program Is For

This is a fit if your company:

- Is an **Australian registered business (ABN)**
- Installs or services **irrigation / pumps / valves**
- Works with **farms, greenhouses, orchards, landscaping**
- Wants to add **automation + remote control** to projects without building systems from scratch

> Not a retail distribution program. We prefer project-capable local companies.

---

## What We Provide

### 1) Ready-to-Install Control Cabinets (High Value for Australia)
- **Pre-wired, tested**, and field-ready
- Integrated **pump + valve** automation
- Multi-zone sequential irrigation logic
- Options for **solar / off-grid** projects

**Benefit:** Reduce on-site wiring time and mistakes—important in Australia where labor cost is high.

### 2) Modular Control Products (Expandable by Project Needs)
- Pump start/stop control (relay or VFD interface)
- Multi-zone scheduling and sequential irrigation
- LoRa long-range wireless (no internet required)
- Optional sensing for verification (flow/pressure/environment)

### 3) Engineering Support (Remote)
- System architecture guidance
- Wiring diagrams and cabinet layout
- Remote commissioning support
- Project-based configuration (not one-size-fits-all)

- Greenhouses requiring stable pump logic and scheduling
