### Why this demo exists

PKYDrip Online Demo lets serious visitors evaluate the control platform using **demonstration system data** and **historical monitoring data** from PKYDrip test and demonstration controllers — without booking a sales meeting first.

This page is for technical evaluation. Control commands are not available here.

### Online Demo vs Book a Demo

- **Online Demo** (this page): short access form → read-only demonstration dashboard.
- **Book / Request Demo**: human consultation for your project — use [Book a Live Demo](/book-a-demo/) or [Contact](/contact/).
