# Data Deletion Request

This page provides instructions on how to request data deletion for **PKYDrip**. We are committed to safeguarding your data and ensuring compliance with privacy laws. 

## Steps to Request Data Deletion

  1. Open the **PKYDrip** application on your device.
  2. Go to **Settings** > **Privacy Settings**.
  3. Click on **Request Data Deletion** and confirm your request.
  4. If you cannot access the app, you can email us at [customerservice@pky-dripirrigation.com](mailto:customerservice@pky-dripirrigation.com) with your account details.

## Types of Data Deleted

  * Personal information (e.g., name, email address).
  * Account details and preferences.
  * Usage history and associated app data.

## Types of Data Retained

  * Transaction records required for legal or tax purposes.
  * Anonymized or aggregated data for analytics, retained for up to 30 days.
