## Typical Irrigation Options for a 10-Acre Farm

Every 10-acre farm is different.  
Some farmers want a **simple and affordable system**, while others want **more automation and future expansion**.

Below are three **typical system options** to help you understand what is possible.  
These are **reference examples**, not fixed packages.

---

## Option A – Basic Automatic Irrigation

**Best for:**  
Farmers who want to replace manual watering and keep the initial cost low.

**What this option includes:**
- Zoned irrigation (irrigates one area at a time)  
- Automatic on/off by timer  
- Basic pump control  
- Drip or sprinkler irrigation  

**Why farmers choose this:**
- Easy to use  
- Much cheaper than irrigating the whole farm at once  
- Can be upgraded later  

**Upgrade ready:**  
You can add more zones or remote control in the future.

---

## Option B – Standard Smart Irrigation

**Best for:**  
Farmers who want better control and less daily involvement.

**What this option includes:**
- Everything in Option A  
- More flexible irrigation schedules  
- Zone-level control  
- Optional remote control (phone or computer)  

**Why farmers choose this:**
- Saves time  
- Easier farm management  
- Suitable for mixed crops  

**Upgrade ready:**  
Sensors and fertilizer systems can be added later without changing the main system.

---

## Option C – Smart & Expandable Irrigation System

**Best for:**  
Farmers planning long-term development or multiple crop types.

**What this option includes:**
- Everything in Option B  
- Remote monitoring and control  
- Support for sensors (soil moisture, water flow)  
- Ready for fertilizer injection system  

**Why farmers choose this:**
- Higher automation  
- Better water control  
- Built for expansion and future needs  

This option is designed to grow with your farm.

---

## Start Simple, Upgrade When Ready

You do **not** need to choose the most advanced option from the beginning.

Many farmers start with **Option A or B**, then upgrade step by step:
- Add remote control later  
- Add more zones when expanding  
- Add sensors or fertilizer systems when needed  

The main system stays the same.

---

## Custom Design for Your Farm

These options are only examples.

Your final system depends on:
- Crop type  
- Water source  
- Power supply (electricity or solar)  
- Budget plan  

We design the system **around your farm**, not a fixed package.
