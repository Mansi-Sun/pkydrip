## What PKYDrip Does Not Supply Directly

PKYDrip does not primarily manufacture hydroponic grow channels, PVC pipes, grow racks, or reservoirs.

These parts are often easier to source locally because dimensions, materials, shipping cost, and installation methods vary by greenhouse, crop, and local market.

PKYDrip focuses on the control system, nutrient dosing logic, EC/pH control, monitoring, irrigation timing, alarm records, and integration support.
