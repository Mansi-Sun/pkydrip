## Deployment Scenarios

Start from how your farm operates — not from equipment or product catalogs.

PKYDrip deployment scenarios describe **how core systems** (irrigation control, fertigation, climate control) are applied in different farm environments. Each scenario connects to system architecture and regional market configurations.
