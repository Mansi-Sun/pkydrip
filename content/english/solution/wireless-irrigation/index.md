## Why Traditional Systems Don’t Work in Remote Fields

- No electricity available in the field  
- Cable installation cost is too high  
- Difficult to expand irrigation zones  
- Long-distance control becomes unreliable  

---

## A Simple Wireless Irrigation Solution

Our system uses LoRa wireless technology to control irrigation devices across large distances.

### System Structure:

- LoRa Gateway (central controller)  
- Solar-powered valve controllers  
- Long-range wireless communication
