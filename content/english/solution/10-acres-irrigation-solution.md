## Irrigation Solution for a 10-Acre Farm

If you have a **10-acre farm** and are planning to install irrigation, this solution is designed to help you **start easily**, **control costs**, and **upgrade step by step** as your farm grows.

You don’t need to install everything at once.  
We design the system based on **your crops, your water source, and your budget**.

---

## How the System Works (Simple Explanation)

Instead of watering the whole farm at the same time, we divide the land into **several irrigation zones**.

Each zone is watered **one by one**, automatically.

This method:
- Keeps water pressure stable  
- Reduces pump size  
- Saves electricity or fuel  
- Makes the system easier to manage

The system can be customized for:

## Easy to Start, Easy to Upgrade

You can start with a **basic setup**, such as:
- Automatic irrigation timing  
- Simple zone control  

Later, you can upgrade by adding:
- Remote control (phone or computer)  
- More irrigation zones  
- Sensors (soil moisture, water flow)  
- Fertilizer injection system  

No need to replace the main system when upgrading.
