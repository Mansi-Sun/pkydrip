## 3) Operating Principle (Safe & Simple)

1. Pump starts first  
2. Mainline becomes pressurized  
3. Valves open sequentially (zone by zone)  
4. After the last zone finishes, the pump stops  
5. If flow or pressure is abnormal → automatic shutdown  

This approach helps you:

- Maintain stable pressure at the field
- Avoid oversizing the pump
- Reduce energy consumption
- Protect the irrigation network (avoid pipe burst / dry run)

---

## 4) Why No Battery Still Works

In this design, **water storage replaces electrical storage**:

- Solar pumping mainly happens during daytime
- Water is stored in a tank or pond
- Irrigation uses stored water
- Level switch prevents overflow and dry-run risk

This reduces cost and long-term maintenance.

---

## 5) Protection & Monitoring (Recommended)

For farms above ~20 acres, we recommend adding at least one verification sensor:

- **Flow verification**: detect dry run, pipe damage, valve failure
- **Pressure monitoring**: detect blockage, burst pipe, abnormal pressure

Key idea:  
**Pump ON does not always mean water is delivered.**  
Flow/pressure closes the verification loop.
