## How the System Works

A typical PKYDrip irrigation control structure can include:

**Water Source**  
↓  
**Pump / Bore Pump**  
↓  
**Filtration System**  
↓  
**PKYDrip Irrigation Controller**  
↓  
**Solenoid Valves**  
↓  
**Multiple Irrigation Zones**

This structure helps automate pump operation and irrigation zones, while making it easier to upgrade existing irrigation systems without rebuilding everything from the beginning.
