## Common Problems in Remote Irrigation Projects

If your irrigation area is far from your control point, you may face:

- No electricity available in the field  
- High cost for trenching and cable installation  
- Long distances between valves and control system  
- Difficult expansion when adding new zones  

These issues often lead to higher costs, more manual work, and limited system flexibility.

---

## A Simpler Solution: LoRa Wireless Irrigation

Instead of building complex wired systems, a LoRa-based irrigation system allows you to control your field wirelessly.

Typical system includes:

- Central LoRa gateway (main controller)  
- Wireless valve controllers in the field  
- Solar-powered operation (no grid required)  
- Multi-zone irrigation scheduling  

This approach reduces installation cost and makes the system easier to expand later.

---

## What You Can Control

With a wireless irrigation system, you can:

- Open and close valves remotely  
- Start and stop water pumps  
- Control multiple irrigation zones  
- Set automatic irrigation schedules  
- Reduce manual field operations
