## Irrigation Design Wizard for New Farms and Projects

Planning a new irrigation system can be difficult without a clear starting point.

Many farm owners and contractors know the land area or crop type, but are unsure about:

- which irrigation method to choose
- how much water is required
- how to size the pump
- how many irrigation zones are needed
- how to structure the system

This guided wizard helps you organize your project step by step and generate a basic irrigation planning direction.

* * *

## What This Wizard Will Help You Estimate

Based on your inputs, the system can provide a basic planning direction:

- approximate water demand
- suggested irrigation method
- estimated pump flow range
- recommended number of irrigation zones
- basic control system structure
- possible upgrade path

This is not a final engineering design, but a practical starting point for discussion and system planning.

* * *

## Why This Wizard Matters

A clear irrigation plan helps you:

- avoid oversizing pumps
- avoid underestimating water demand
- design proper irrigation zones
- reduce system cost
- plan future upgrades (automation, fertigation, sensors)

This wizard is designed as a practical engineering starting point — not a theoretical tool.
