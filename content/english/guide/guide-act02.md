# Irrigation System Guide – ACT02  
## Pump-Aware Irrigation Control (Package B / C)

ACT02 is designed **only for irrigation systems with water pumps**.  
It focuses on **pump control, protection, pressure stability, and VFD integration**.

> **ACT01 helps you start irrigation.**  
> **ACT02 helps you protect and control water systems.**

If your irrigation system includes a water pump, ACT02 is recommended.

---

## Who Should Use ACT02

This guide is suitable if **any of the following applies**:

- Your irrigation system uses a **water pump**
- You want the pump to **start and stop automatically** with irrigation
- You want to **protect the pump** from dry run or abnormal conditions
- Water pressure is **unstable or difficult to manage**
- You are using or planning to use a **VFD (Variable Frequency Drive)**
- The system is designed for **agriculture, orchards, or engineering projects**

ACT02 is **not designed for hobby gardens or valve-only systems**.

## Recommendation Logic (ACT02 Output)

ACT02 provides **only one of the following two results**.

---

## Package B – Pump + Valve Control (Core)

### When This Package Is Recommended
- Automatic pump start/stop is required  
- VFD is not mandatory  
- Basic pressure or flow protection is sufficient  

### Description
**Package B** links irrigation zones with automatic pump operation.  
The pump starts and stops together with irrigation zones, with **basic pressure and flow monitoring** to protect against dry run or abnormal conditions.

This package is suitable for farms and irrigation systems that require **reliable automation without complex variable-speed control**.

### Key Characteristics
- Automatic pump and valve linkage  
- Basic pump protection  
- Cost-effective and field-proven  
- Suitable for drip and sprinkler systems  

---

## Package C – Pump + VFD + Feedback (Advanced)

### When This Package Is Recommended
- VFD is required or already installed  
- Solar or hybrid power system is used  
- Medium to large system scale  
- Pressure stability and energy efficiency are critical  

### Description
**Package C** integrates **pressure and flow sensors with VFD control**, enabling soft start, energy saving, and full pump protection under variable operating conditions.

This configuration is designed for **professional agricultural and engineering-grade irrigation projects**.

### Key Characteristics
- Pressure-aware irrigation control  
- VFD-ready integration  
- Soft start and reduced mechanical stress  
- Energy-efficient operation  
- Engineering-grade reliability
