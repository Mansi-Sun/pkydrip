Modern irrigation systems are used across many agricultural environments, each with different requirements for water supply, pressure stability, irrigation timing, and system scalability.

PKYDrip provides modular irrigation control solutions that can be adapted to a wide range of agricultural and landscape irrigation projects.

This section introduces **typical irrigation applications** where smart irrigation control systems can improve efficiency, reduce labor, and simplify irrigation management.

---

## Typical Irrigation Applications

### Open Field Irrigation

Open-field irrigation systems are commonly used for large agricultural areas where one pump must supply water to multiple irrigation zones.

These systems require reliable **sequential valve control**, stable pump operation, and structured irrigation scheduling.

Typical crops include:

- vegetables  
- field crops  
- large-scale agricultural planting

Future content will explain system architecture, zoning strategies, and pump coordination for open-field irrigation projects.

---

### Orchard Irrigation

Orchards often require carefully planned irrigation zones to ensure consistent water supply for tree crops.

Typical orchard irrigation features include:

- drip irrigation systems  
- pressure-regulated emitters  
- multiple irrigation blocks  
- seasonal irrigation adjustments

PKYDrip controllers help automate irrigation scheduling while allowing growers to manage multiple orchard sections more easily.

---

### Greenhouse Irrigation

Greenhouse irrigation systems require higher precision compared to open-field agriculture.

These systems may involve:

- drip irrigation lines  
- climate-controlled irrigation schedules  
- fertigation integration  
- sensor-driven irrigation control

Automation improves irrigation consistency and supports more advanced greenhouse crop management.

---

### Hydroponic Irrigation

Hydroponic systems require precise control of water and nutrient delivery.

Typical hydroponic irrigation structures may include:

- nutrient mixing tanks  
- EC and pH monitoring  
- irrigation cycles based on time or sensor input  
- automated nutrient dosing systems

PKYDrip systems can be integrated with fertigation controllers to support modern hydroponic growing environments.

---

### Landscape Irrigation

Landscape irrigation systems are commonly used in:

- parks  
- residential communities  
- golf courses  
- public green spaces

These projects often require:

- sprinkler irrigation  
- underground sprinkler systems  
- programmable irrigation schedules  
- water-saving control strategies

Smart irrigation controllers allow landscape managers to automate irrigation schedules and reduce water waste.

---

## Modular System Architecture

Although irrigation environments differ, many irrigation systems share a similar modular architecture.

Typical irrigation systems include:

- water source  
- pump system  
- filtration equipment  
- irrigation controller  
- zone valves  
- irrigation terminals (drip, sprinkler, misting)

This modular design allows irrigation systems to scale from smaller farms to larger agricultural projects.

---

## Engineering-Oriented Irrigation Solutions

PKYDrip irrigation control systems are designed to support **project-based irrigation automation**, rather than only simple household irrigation.

This platform approach allows:

- integration of multiple irrigation zones  
- coordination between pumps and valves
