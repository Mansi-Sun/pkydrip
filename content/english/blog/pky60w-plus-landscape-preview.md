**PKYDRIP PKY60W-Plus** is a landscape irrigation controller for gardens, green space, courtyards and nurseries. Prototype development is complete. Experimental-site and field tests are underway in mainland China. This page is a product preview and a call for regional field-test partners — not an in-stock production catalogue.

<p class="pky60w-plus-note"><strong>Start with country or region, plus valve type.</strong> Zone count, pump data and purchase quantity can follow. You do not need a full specification sheet to enquire.</p>

## What you can specify now

| Capability | What it means for the project |
|---|---|
| Two-wire encoder / decoder | Plan valves and decoder addresses on a two-wire path. Compatible decoder, cable length, wire size and load are confirmed per project. Two-wire decoding is not a radio band. |
| Valve outputs | Match AC 24 V, DC 24 V or latching valves by configuration. One output is not a mix-and-match port. |
| Pump start | Pump-start control through a starter, contactor or matching interface. Pump-start control does not mean the controller carries motor current. |
| Local + phone + remote | 4.3-inch touchscreen included; 7-inch is discussable. Wi-Fi + Bluetooth phone setup, MQTT and cloud remote control, subject to network, configuration and account rights. |
| Water records | A matched flow sensor records water use so you can check programs and improve schedules. No promised saving percentage. |

<p class="pky60w-plus-warn">Items not listed here — model mix, electrical ratings, IP rating, zone count, range, price and lead time — are confirmed per project. This preview is not a specification sheet.</p>

## Regional field tests

We invite landscape installers, integrators, wholesalers, suppliers and regional distributors to test local valves, pumps, sensors, two-wire decoder installation, screen/phone/cloud use, and water-use records.

Overseas trial price and distributor support can be discussed. Mainland China tests do not mean every market is already adapted or certified. We do not claim global certification, any-decoder compatibility, or that every function ships as standard.

Wireless band, gateway and local rules are confirmed per country. That work is separate from two-wire decoder control.

<div class="pky60w-plus-later">

**Later expansion, not current standard.** LoRaWAN valves, flow and pressure sensors are a follow-on plan. Partners with those projects can share local band, gateway and network needs now. Support, scope and supply dates stay project-confirmed.
