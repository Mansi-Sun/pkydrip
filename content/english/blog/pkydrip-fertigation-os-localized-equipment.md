Developing a fertigation machine involves much more than buying a PLC, pumps, valves and sensors. The most time-consuming work is matching the hardware, implementing control logic, calibrating measurements, handling faults and proving that the complete machine will operate reliably.

**PKYDrip Fertigation OS** combines a PKYDrip control board, verified hardware and sensors, configurable fertigation software, and documented APIs. It gives irrigation companies and equipment manufacturers a tested control core for building localized fertigation machines without creating a complete hardware and software team from the beginning.

## A complete control system, not a standalone PCB

The commercial product is not simply a circuit board. It is a controlled combination of:

- PKYDrip control hardware and expansion interfaces;
- field-tested and verified pumps, valves and sensors;
- configurable irrigation, dosing and EC/pH functions;
- local operation, alarms, records and protection logic;
- Modbus, MQTT or API integration where required;
- wiring information, configuration documents and technical support.

PKYDrip tests and inspects the equipment in real operating conditions. Verification covers sensor readings, pump and valve interlocks, loss of flow, abnormal pressure, power recovery, EC/pH adjustment, parameter storage, alarms and continuous operation.

The result is a repeatable control system with defined interfaces. The partner can focus on the local hydraulic design, enclosure, electrical cabinet, installation and customer service.

## Case 1: assembling a system from separately sourced components

An irrigation company can purchase pumps, pipes, a PLC, valves, flow sensors and pressure sensors separately from the Chinese supply market. This approach may reduce the initial component price, but every part still has to be selected, connected, programmed and tested as one machine.

Signal types and operating ranges may not match. Sensor errors, unstable control sequences and incomplete protection logic often appear only during commissioning. The company must then use software, electrical and testing personnel to diagnose the complete system.

By choosing PKYDrip-verified control hardware, sensors and software, a partner can build the equipment under its own brand and concentrate on localization and site commissioning. Depending on project complexity, this can shorten development and validation by approximately **three to six months**, while also reducing software development and hardware-debugging costs.

## Case 2: purchasing a fixed PLC fertigation machine

A conventional PLC machine is normally programmed for its original crop plan, pumps, sensors and operating sequence. When the crop, nutrient recipe, pump arrangement or sensor set changes, the customer may need the original supplier to modify the program and perform another commissioning cycle.

That waiting time can delay equipment delivery and, in serious cases, affect the planting schedule.

PKYDrip Fertigation OS uses configurable channels, recipes, sensors, target values and control sequences. Adding or replacing a supported sensor normally requires a configuration change rather than a new control program. Software can be updated online or by USB, reducing repeated development work and long-term maintenance costs.

## API delivery instead of source-code complexity

Most equipment companies do not need to maintain the source code of an entire fertigation platform. Source-code delivery introduces version control, development-team requirements and unclear responsibility for future faults.

PKYDrip therefore provides configuration tools and documented interfaces instead. Standard Modbus, MQTT or API connections allow the fertigation controller to exchange data with a local HMI, a customer platform or other agricultural equipment.

This approach creates a clearer commercial relationship:

1. the partner does not need to build a full software department;
2. PKYDrip remains responsible for the core control system and maintained versions;
3. integration boundaries and after-sales responsibilities remain clear.

PKYDrip Fertigation OS is intended for irrigation integrators, greenhouse engineering companies, fertigation-machine manufacturers, automation companies and distributors developing their own equipment range. It is not positioned as a DIY controller for an undefined one-off experiment.

Standard hardware and software come first. Branding, enclosure changes, special interfaces and private deployment are available after technical review and are subject to MOQ and commercial terms.
