PKYDrip is structured as a **system-first platform**, not a product catalog.

**One system → Multiple markets → Multiple deployment scenarios.**

The three core systems — irrigation control, fertigation, and climate control — adapt to regional climate conditions and farm types. Wireless field expansion, solar pumping, and monitoring are supporting modules within deployment scenarios, not separate entry points.

Start from [core systems](/system/irrigation-control-system/), then choose a [regional market](/ru/) or [deployment scenario](/solution/).
