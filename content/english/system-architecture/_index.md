PKYDrip irrigation automation systems are designed around real irrigation logic: water source, pump, filtration, fertigation, valves, sensors, wireless field control, and monitoring.

Monitoring & Sensors is treated as a supporting module and cross-system capability. It can support irrigation control, fertigation, wireless irrigation, and solar pump irrigation projects, but it is not a fifth primary System in the V2 structure.

Start from the system architecture first, then choose the core system, application page, and product modules that fit your project.
