PKYDrip demonstration systems are designed to help customers understand how irrigation controllers, valves, pumps, and fertigation equipment work together in real irrigation environments.

These demo systems are commonly used for:

- showroom demonstrations
- training sessions
- technical presentations
- irrigation project discussions

For live demonstration data and a Zoom equipment meeting, visit [PKYDRIP Live Demo](/live-demo/). This directory describes physical demonstration configurations.
