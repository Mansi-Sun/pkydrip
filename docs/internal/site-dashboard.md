## Current Structure Signals

- The site already has the future vocabulary (`Systems`, `Solutions`, `Products`) but system-level pages still exist in several locations.
- Products are doing two jobs: real product module pages and system/family hub pages.
- Resources are fragmented across docs, guides, guide, tools, downloads, internal, strategy, and older service/FAQ pages.
- Landing pages are numerous and mostly appropriate as campaign pages outside main navigation.
- Layout count is higher than the target V2 model and should be standardized later after content roles are confirmed.
