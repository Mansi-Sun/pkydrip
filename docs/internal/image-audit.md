## Recommendations

- System pages should favor architecture diagrams, dashboards, and application context over isolated product photos.
- Product pages should keep product images, but add installation/application visuals and a simple system integration diagram where practical.
- Solution pages should show customer scenario photos first, then recommended architecture and product modules.
- Landing pages should use campaign-specific hero visuals and simple control-flow diagrams; avoid looking like generic product catalog pages.
- Resource pages with no visuals should gradually receive explanatory diagrams or screenshots when they support engineering education or SEO.
