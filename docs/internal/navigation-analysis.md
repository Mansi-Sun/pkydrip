## Navigation Findings

- Current main navigation is already close to the proposed V2 top-level model, but `Resources` is absent from the main menu even though docs, tools, downloads, guides, demos, and strategy pages exist.
- Product pages currently contain both real product modules and system-level family pages. This makes the Products entry carry too much structural responsibility.
- Footer includes Download and FAQ, but docs/tools/guides are not surfaced as one coherent Resources area.
- Landing and partner pages are intentionally outside main navigation, which is appropriate for campaign pages.
