Fertigation Equipment & Supply Questions

What fertigation equipment can I order?

PKYDrip offers multi-channel fertigation machines, peristaltic dosing equipment and the FG301 three-channel control package. Complete equipment and control-only packages have different supply scopes; confirm the selected configuration in the quotation.

Does EC/pH monitoring include automatic dosing control?

Monitoring and automatic closed-loop control are different configurations. Confirm the required functions, compatible dosing equipment and sensor inclusion before ordering.

Can we use our own logo?

Yes. Logo placement, labels and the approved branding scope are agreed before production. New interface or software work is quoted separately where needed.

Can we start with a sample?

Ask for a sample quotation for the selected configuration. Sample price, availability, branding requirements and repeat-order quantities are confirmed in writing.

Is FG301 supplied as a complete pump skid?

The FG301 control package excludes the water pump and equipment frame. If you need complete fertigation equipment, request that supply format explicitly.

Do we receive source code or our own cloud platform?

The standard offer is equipment and agreed platform access. Source-code rights, separate cloud deployments and private-label apps require a separate written agreement.
