Before You Apply

We select partners by market fit and delivery capability

Move from development risk to market testing

Independent development can take years of equipment selection, software work, debugging and production validation. It also requires a stable technical team, and staff turnover can interrupt the product roadmap.

PKYDrip combines verified hardware, a mature supply chain, modular software and open interfaces. Suitable projects can move from confirmed requirements to a working prototype in months — and selected configurations may be ready for market evaluation in weeks.

Cooperation can start under an NDA with PKYDrip customization and manufacturing. Under a separate written agreement, partners can gradually add their own brand, functions and intellectual property, and later discuss customer-managed assembly or production.

  • Dealers may focus on sales and customer development.
  • Authorized service providers need implementation and installation capability.
  • Regional partners need sales, implementation, installation and after-sales capability.
  • White-label development requires a defined specification, business plan, MOQ and written agreement.
Initial cooperation is non-exclusive.

Territory or white-label market protection must match actual sales volume, service coverage, investment and agreed targets.
