A Modular Irrigation Control Architecture

PKYDrip is built around a layered irrigation system architecture rather than a fixed product bundle. This helps customers plan new projects, upgrade existing systems, and expand step by step without rebuilding everything.

The structure can include sensing, communication, execution, and decision layers — making it easier to integrate pumps, valves, fertigation, and remote control logic into one scalable system.

Decision & Control Layer

Coordinates irrigation schedules, zone logic, pump and valve linkage, fertigation timing, and remote monitoring.

Communication Layer

Connects field devices through wired or wireless communication, including LoRa, RS485, and gateway-based expansion.

Execution Layer

Handles physical actions such as valve control, pump operation, fertigation execution, and irrigation zone control.

Data Sensing Layer

Collects soil moisture, pressure, flow, EC/pH, temperature, humidity, and other field data for better irrigation decisions.

This layered structure allows old systems to be upgraded, new systems to be planned more clearly, and future expansion to be carried out with less disruption.
