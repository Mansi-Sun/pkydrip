Automation

One Control System for Pumps and Valves

The control cabinet links pumps and irrigation valves together. It can start the pump, open irrigation zones, monitor operation status, and improve field management efficiency.

  • Pump start / stop control
  • Sequential or group irrigation
  • Valve interlock protection
  • Pressure and flow monitoring options

Zone logic

From One Pump to Multiple Irrigation Zones

One pump can serve multiple irrigation zones. The control system opens valves one by one according to the irrigation schedule, helping reduce labor and protect the pump from overload.

Pump Station → Control Cabinet → Zone 1 → Zone 2 → Zone 3 → Zone 4
